**m6 fix (label typography splits by markup accident):** the audit found "How I Operate" rendering in Lora when tagged `h2` while the sidebar labels render in Inter as `<p>`. **Resolution: ALL uppercase tracked labels render in Inter via `--text-label`, regardless of HTML tag.** "How I Operate" stays semantically an `<h2>` (it heads a self-contained module) but is *styled* with `--text-label` (Inter 700, 0.18em), not the Lora heading default. This gives the page exactly two label-vs-heading visual roles, each one typeface (refactoring_ui §7: as many families as you need, no more).

**m6 secondary (subordination):** the audit noted "How I Operate" (small label) sits visually subordinate to sibling "My Family" (larger heading), which is correct *now* that "How I Operate" is a uniform small label and "My Family" is a section heading — the hierarchy is intentional, not accidental. ## 3. The sidebar (Task 1b — CrossFit credential placement; TFA addition)

### 3.1 Credentials list — exact placement and type (Task 1b core)

The content draft sets the four-item list. **Spec the order, type, and the CrossFit line's treatment:**

```
Credentials                         ← --text-label, Inter 600, neutral-600, 0.18em
PhD, Educational Policy Studies      ← --text-base, Inter 500, neutral-800
  (UVA, IES Fellow)                    parenthetical same line, neutral-600 weight 400
MPP (Vanderbilt, Peabody Honors Scholar)
Excellence in Teaching Award,        ← NEW (TFA, content draft Edit 4)
  Teach For America South Louisiana (2013)
Certified CrossFit Trainer (CF-L3)   ← the fitness consolidation landing line
```

- **CrossFit line type treatment (Task 1b — the explicit ask):** the CF-L3 credential renders as a **peer list item, identical in weight and size to the academic credentials** (`--text-base`, Inter 500, `neutral-800` at 13.9:1). It is **not** visually demoted, badged, or color-differentiated — it sits as one credential among four. This is the whole point of the consolidation: CrossFit (CF-L3, top ~1% of coaches; canonical) becomes a **one-line credibility signal**, not a content vertical. One line, no flourish, equal treatment. The parenthetical "(CF-L3)" carries the selectivity shorthand; the canonical "top ~1% of CrossFit coaches" detail lives on the Personal page, not in the sidebar (sidebar stays scannable).
- **Order rationale:** academic (PhD, MPP) → professional-teaching recognition (TFA) → athletic (CF-L3). Groups by domain, descends in conventional resume priority. The content draft offered the Designer the reorder call; **this order is the spec.**
- **Density (carried from the 2026-05-03 open question #2):** four entries, two of which wrap to a second line (PhD parenthetical, TFA full title). Set the list `space-y-2` with `leading-snug` per item so the two-line entries breathe. On a 13" `lg` display this stays above the fold; if the Auditor flags overflow, the fallback is to shorten "MPP (Vanderbilt, Peabody Honors Scholar)" to "MPP (Vanderbilt)" and move Peabody Honors to the Experience Education block. **Do not** shorten the CF-L3 or TFA lines (they're the cycle's additions and each is already one logical unit).

### 3.3 Family content home — the consolidation decision (Gate 1 flag §0.1)

**Designer recommendation: family paragraph + 3-photo grid STAY on About.** Reasoning, tied to the audience priority (strategic_brief §Audiences):

- The About page is where audience 5 (staff/scholars) and any reader seeking "the human presence behind the work" expect family. The narrative's final movement is *meant* to land on family ("Being his dad is the best job I have"). Removing it amputates the page's emotional close.
- Pattern 13 (greenlit) moves **fitness** off the site and consolidates the **fitness credential** to one sidebar line. It does **not** require stripping family from About. The "quieter dedicated home" the prompt asks me to pick is for the *fitness/personal-vertical* content, which the Personal page absorbs (see personal spec). Family on About + an expanded Personal page are complementary, not duplicative: About carries the brief, warm version; Personal carries the fuller version.
- This keeps the positioning real estate (hero, About opening, the technical paragraph) uncluttered while preserving the human close — exactly the Pattern 13 intent ("family content adjacent to positioning real estate competes; a quieter home serves better").

**m10 fix (family photos):** the three photos currently use `transition-transform hover:scale-[1.02]`. **Remove the hover scale** — the photos are not interactive and the hover implies clickability (m10; global spec §4.3). Keep the `transition-all duration-500` only if it serves a fade-in on load; otherwise render static. Photos keep `overflow-hidden rounded-lg shadow-sm`. **Image treatment (refactoring_ui §10):** `h-48 w-full object-cover`, `rounded-lg`, `shadow-sm` at rest, no border (border + shadow compete — §9, pick one; shadow wins for photos). Aspect: the `h-48` fixed height + `object-cover` gives a consistent ~3:2 landscape crop across all three. Alt text kept (`Preston with Liza`, `Family Halloween photo`, `Franklin`). **Family photos are the only people-images permitted** (constraint confirmed; no scholars).

**If Preston picks "relocate family to Personal":** About keeps the family *sentence* (one line in the narrative close) + the "Based in Washington, DC" line; the 3-photo grid moves to the Personal page (personal spec §3 already accommodates it). The "My Family" `<h2>` and grid are removed from About; the narrative simply ends on the family sentence. This is the cleaner-for-executives variant; I default to the recommendation above but the layout supports either.

## 6. Spacing & rhythm

- Container `max-w-7xl`, page padding `px-6 py-16 lg:px-8 lg:py-24` (kept).
- Body paragraph spacing: `space-y-8` (kept; the content draft adds no new paragraph this cycle — it edits existing ones). The 2026-05-03 bump to `space-y-9` is **not needed** this cycle since paragraph count is unchanged.
- Sidebar internal `space-y-8` (kept). Credentials list `space-y-2` + `leading-snug` (§3.1).
- Family section: `pt-8 border-t border-neutral-200` (kept) — the section break uses a neutral hairline, not burgundy (burgundy budget reserved for nav). Photo grid `gap-4 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3` (kept).
- **Body line length:** the narrative `<article>` is `lg:col-span-2` of `max-w-7xl` ≈ wider than optimal 65–75ch. **Add `max-w-prose` (65ch) to the paragraph container** so body lines don't exceed ~75 characters (refactoring_ui §6; current line length on `lg` runs long). The dek and H1 may exceed prose width (they're display text). This is the one rhythm refinement that materially improves readability.

## 8. Accessibility (this surface)

- **Headings:** one H1. "My Family" = H2. "How I Operate" = H2 (styled as a label but semantically a section heading; no level skip H1→H2). Operating-principle labels are `<strong>` inside `<li>`, not headings (correct). Confirm no H1→H3 jump (mdn_semantic_html).
- **Landmarks:** `<article>` (narrative) + `<aside>` (sidebar) inside the single Layout `<main>`. Page wrapper is `<div>`, not a second `<main>` (M5).
- **Focus:** global `:focus-visible` (2px primary ring, 2px offset) on the location link and any body link. Sidebar has no interactive elements beyond inherited links.
- **Images:** all three family photos have alt text (kept). They are content (figure-class), not decorative, so non-empty alt is correct (wcag_2.2_aa §1.1.1).
- **Arrows/checkmarks:** `aria-hidden="true"` (m17).
- **Contrast:** every pairing ≥7.2:1 (§5). Passes 1.4.3 AA with margin.
