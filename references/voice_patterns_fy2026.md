## Voice rules reminder (from preston-writing v2.5)

Before any snippet lands on the site:
- No "equity" language
- No "X, not Y" / "Not X, but Y" / "This isn't X — it's Y"
- No "passionate about," "driven by," "on a mission to," "believes in"
- No em-dash overuse (budget: ~1 per 200 words)
- Active voice
- Lead with the point
- Names the artifact + the number; doesn't pad
- Pattern 14 discipline: "we" for org outcomes, "I" only for genuinely solo work
