# Evals — Resume Consultant

What "good" looks like: prose in Preston's voice, every number canonical, zero anti-AI tells, correct credit attribution and capability tense. Pairs are AI-generic input → Preston-voice output.

## Worked I/O pairs

**1. AI-generic hero line → Preston voice.**
Input: "Preston is a passionate, data-driven leader on a mission to transform educational equity through innovative solutions."
Output: "I design governance and build the analytic tools that drive strategy and decisions." (Strips "passionate/driven/on a mission," removes "equity," leads with a verb and an artifact.)

**2. Credential-led → outcome-led with selectivity.**
Input: "PhD-trained researcher who led research at Common App."
Output: "Founding member of the research function at Common App, the largest college application dataset in the United States." (Pattern 12: outcome before credential; names the selectivity per canonical § Selectivity Language.)

**3. Capability-tense fix.**
Input: "CPIP is an AI platform that matches students to career pathways."
Output: "I'm building CPIP, a recommendation engine that will match students to career-connected pathways (October 2026 launch)." (CPIP is not yet live — future/progressive.)

## Edge cases

**A. Team outcome in first person (Pattern 14).** Input: "I drove our partners to 75-95% graduation." → "At DC CAP, graduation runs 75-95% at established partners" — org outcome is DC CAP's, not Preston's.

**B. Forbidden construction.** A draft reads "This isn't a data role, it's a strategy role." → Rewrite as a positive claim; the "isn't X, it's Y" form is a hard-banned AI tell.

**C. Unverifiable number Preston wants.** Preston asks to cite "#1 in DC." Not in canonical. → Route to canonical-update through the Site Lead; drop the claim until verified. Never approximate.
