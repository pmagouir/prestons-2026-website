summary: "When I arrived at DC CAP, I ran a rigorous evaluation of our university partnerships across several retention milestones and model specifications. The evidence drove a strategic pivot to a regional partnership model: concentrate in the Mid-Atlantic, enforce 80%+ retention and debt caps, and exit underperforming partners. On average, 76% of the Class of 2021 earned a degree within five years, with partner campuses ranging from 57% to 100% (the 100% at West Chester, a former partner). Seven partners reached 100% first-to-second-year retention in AY2025–26."
